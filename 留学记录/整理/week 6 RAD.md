### 3. When AI tracks our location or maps our skills to help us, how do we stop it from becoming a surveillance tool?(当AI追踪我们的位置或映射我们的技能来帮助我们时，我们如何防止它成为一种监控工具？)
* **Use privacy-preserving techniques:** Implement strict privacy protections like differential privacy or federated learning to secure personal data during AI training and computation.
* (使用隐私保护技术：实施严格的隐私保护措施，如差分隐私或联邦学习，以在AI训练和计算期间保护个人数据。)
* **Enforce data minimization and consent:** Ensure that AI systems only collect necessary data with proper user consent, preventing unauthorized access and invasive tracking.
* (强制执行数据最小化和同意原则：确保AI系统在获得用户适当同意的情况下仅收集必要的数据，防止未经授权的访问和侵入式追踪。)




* **Maintain human oversight:** Keep humans in the loop to monitor how spatial or personal data is being used, ensuring that tracking does not cross the line into unauthorized surveillance.
* (保持人工监督：让人类参与其中，监督空间或个人数据的长远使用情况，确保追踪行为不会越界变成未授权的监控。)

### 4. Is it more important for an AI model to be highly accurate, or is it more important for it to be completely fair?**(对于一个AI模型来说，是高准确率更重要，还是完全公平更重要？)**

* **Both dimensions are essential, but trade-offs exist:** Building trustworthy AI requires balancing multiple pillars, where focusing solely on high predictive accuracy can sometimes hide deep structural or demographic biases.
* (两个维度都很重要，但存在权衡：构建可信赖的AI需要平衡多个支柱，如果仅仅关注高预测准确率，有时会掩盖深层的结构性或人口学偏见。)

* **Fairness prevents harmful discrimination:** Ensuring "Fairness" is critical because an accurate model that discriminates against minority groups or dark-skinned faces can cause severe real-world harm.
* (公平性可以防止有害的歧视：确保“公平性”至关重要，因为一个歧视少数群体或深色皮肤面孔的准确模型会在现实世界中造成严重的危害。)
